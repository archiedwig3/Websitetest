Welcome to Odyssey Online
New Eden

Join the world of New Eden today and create up to six unique characters, each hailing from a variety of distinct classes, all offering different perks and magical abilities to suit your playstyle.

Embark on quests with friends and level up your skills. Master the craft of a Blacksmith to forge powerful weapons, shields, and armor, or become an Alchemist, specialising in creating magical potions for healing and spell enhancements.

Engage in epic battles against monsters and rival players alike. Join an existing Guild, or establish your own, inviting fellow adventurers to unite with you on your journey toward dominance in the world of New Eden!

For the best experience, we recommend playing in Google Chrome. The game runs most smoothly and reliably there.

About Odyssey Online Classic

The Odyssey Online Classic: New Eden is a free-to-play top down 2D MMORPG which has been around since 1997.

New Eden is currently a work in progress and is based on a previous compiled, executable version of the Odyssey Online Classic developed, maintained and hosted by James Chambers (Remote). Special thanks to all involved in keeping this game alive.
