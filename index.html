Welcome to Odyssey

Midveil

Join the world of Midveil and create up to six unique characters, each hailing from a variety of distinct classes. All offering different perks and magical abilities to suit your playstyle.

Embark on quests with friends and level up your skills. Master the craft of a Blacksmith to forge powerful weapons, shields, and armor, or become an Alchemist, specializing in creating magical potions for healing and spell enhancements.

Engage in epic battles against monsters and rival players alike in this persistent open world.
